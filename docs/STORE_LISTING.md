**Name:** VisionGuard
(if the name is already taken in a store: `VisionGuard by Smart Way`)

**Subtitle (Apple):** Body-worn camera control

**Short description (Google, 71 characters):** Watch body-worn cameras live, handle alarms and review recordings.

**Promotional text (Apple):** Your cameras, your alarms and your docking stations in one app. Sign in with the same account you use on the VisionGuard web dashboard.

**Description:**
VisionGuard is the mobile app for owners and supervisors of body-worn camera fleets run on the VisionGuard platform by Smart Way Group.

See what matters, wherever you are:
- Live view: watch any of your cameras live, with the quality adjusting to your network.
- Alarms: SOS and battery alerts appear at once, and you can acknowledge them.
- Map: see where every camera is.
- Fleet: online status, who carries each camera, battery level and recording state.
- Docking stations: the real layout of the dock, which camera sits in which slot, and what it is doing.
- Recordings: browse, play and download the recordings that were offloaded from your cameras.
- Your profile: see your role and exactly what you are allowed to do.
- Support: open a ticket with the Smart Way support team from the app.

Built for companies:
- Use your existing VisionGuard account. You only see the cameras of your own company, and only what your administrator allows you to see and do.
- English and Arabic, with full right-to-left layout.
- Unlock with Face ID or your fingerprint.
- Connections are encrypted.

Accounts are created by your company administrator. The app does not create accounts and has no in-app purchases or advertising.
