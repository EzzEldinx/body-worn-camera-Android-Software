Start of shiftThe employee signs in at the check-out station. The system gives them a charged, ready camera and lights up its bay.

2 · Hardware

The body-worn camera

A rugged camera that clips on the uniform. Large physical buttons are easy to press with gloves, and each important action has its own button.

Buttons

  • Record (green): start and stop recording
  • SOS (orange): emergency alarm
  • Talk: push-to-talk to the control room
  • Photo and flashlight
  • Power (red)

Built in

  • Wide-angle camera with night vision (infrared lights)
  • GPS position, shown on your map
  • Microphone and loudspeaker for talking and voice messages
  • Wi-Fi and mobile data for live video and alerts
  • Metal contacts for charging and copying files on the dock

What it does for you

  • Records and streams. Records in high definition and saves the video on the camera, even with no network. When the network is available it also streams live.
  • Raises the alarm. Hold SOS for two seconds: the control room sees the camera, the place and the officer.
  • Knows who carries it. It prints the date, time, camera number and employee number on the video.*
  • Stays up to date. New software is sent from VisionGuard. Cameras on the dock update themselves.
  • Clips on securely. A strong clip on the back keeps it in place on the uniform.

* Employee stamp and the battery reading on the video are part of the latest camera software. Ask us for the technical datasheet with exact camera specifications.

3 · Hardware

The docking station

Put the camera on the dock and everything happens by itself: it charges, its recordings are copied and checked, and its software is kept up to date.

What the dock does

  • Charges the cameras
  • Copies every recording and photo from the camera
  • Checks each file is perfect (a digital fingerprint is compared)
  • Updates the camera software when needed
  • Shows the real battery of every camera

What you see

  • Which camera is in which bay
  • Progress, for example 4 of 9 files
  • Free space on each camera
  • "Synced" when everything is safely copied
  • A dock marked online or offline
The dock station runs with a small program on the PC next to it. If the PC loses the internet, nothing is lost: files stay on the dock and go to the cloud as soon as the connection returns.

5 · Safety

SOS alarms and battery alerts

The two things that matter most in the field are an officer asking for help and a camera that runs out of power. VisionGuard watches both for you.

SOS alarm

  • Appears on every screen of your company with a sound
  • Shows the camera, the officer, the time and the place on the map
  • One click to open the live video
  • Supervisors with the mobile app are notified on their phones, even locked
  • Press "Acknowledge" and the time you took to answer is recorded

Low battery

  • Every camera shows its battery everywhere in the system
  • Under 20 % and not charging: an alert, a soft beep and a message on the phone
  • Under 10 %: a critical alert
  • The alert clears by itself when the camera is charged
An SOS is never lost. Even if the screen is closed or the internet drops for a moment, the alarm is saved and shown as soon as the control room connects.
6 · Accountability

Who carries which camera

Every employee has a personal login. At the check-out station they receive a ready camera, and VisionGuard records who took which camera, when and for how long.

Simple for employees

Sign in, press "Take", pick the camera from the lit bay. Put it back at the end of the shift and the shift closes by itself. The station signs out after 90 seconds without a touch.

Clear for you

The camera shows its holder on the map and in the app. An SOS remembers who carried the camera when it was pressed. Managers can close a shift manually at any time.

11 · Trust

Security and evidence you can rely on

Video is only useful if it can be trusted. VisionGuard protects it from the camera to the cloud, and shows who touched what.

Every file is checked

A unique digital fingerprint is calculated on the camera, on the dock and again on the server. A file is accepted only if all of them match, so a damaged or changed file is never stored as good.

A sealed audit trail

Every step is recorded: file copied, who decided what to do with it, uploaded, downloaded, deleted from the camera, settings changed. The record cannot be edited or erased, and any tampering is detected.

Your data is yours

Your users see only your cameras, alarms, recordings and messages. Access is checked on the server for every request.

Protected access

Personal logins with roles. Passwords are stored securely, repeated wrong attempts are blocked, and a person's access ends immediately when their account is switched off.

Secure connection

The system is reached over an encrypted connection. Video links are time-limited, so a copied link stops working.

Always recording, never blocked

Alarms and recordings keep arriving even if a subscription has ended, so safety never depends on a payment date.

You decide how long recordings are kept. Files can stay forever, or be removed automatically after a number of days.
